Privacy

Your location is the group's business. Nobody else's.

We don't sell your data and we don't show ads. You can delete your account and all your data from Profile → Delete account at any time.

  • Sign-inWe sign you in with Google and store your name and bike for your profile.
  • LocationShared only with riders in your trip, and only while that trip is active.
  • Your ridesHistory, reports and garage data live in your account and are visible only to you — trip reports are visible to riders in that trip.
  • Third partiesGoogle Firebase (sign-in and data storage), OpenStreetMap (map tiles), openrouteservice (routes and place search).
